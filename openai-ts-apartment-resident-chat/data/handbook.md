# Larkspur Court Resident Handbook (fictional)

Larkspur Court is a 5-floor apartment building with 48 units. The front desk is staffed Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00. Office phone: ext. 100 from the lobby phone.

## Laundry
- The laundry room is in the basement, next to the bike storage.
- Open daily from 7:00 to 22:00. The last wash cycle must start by 21:00.
- 6 washers and 6 dryers. A wash costs $2.50, a dryer cycle costs $1.75. Pay with the Larkspur app wallet or the coin changer by the door.
- Do not leave laundry in a machine more than 20 minutes after the cycle ends. Staff may move it to the folding table.

## Parking
- The garage has 30 spaces for 48 units. Spaces are assigned by lottery each January.
- Residents without a space can rent a visitor-lot permit for $40 per month from the front desk. Permits cannot be used overnight on Fridays (lot is cleaned).
- Visitor parking is free for up to 4 hours. Ask the front desk to register the plate for longer stays, up to 3 nights per month.
- Electric vehicle charging: 2 chargers on level G1, first come first served, 3 hour limit.

## Packages
- Packages are accepted at the front desk during staffed hours and placed in the parcel room on the ground floor.
- After hours, carriers use the 12 smart lockers in the lobby. You receive a text with a 6-digit pickup code.
- Packages are held for 7 days. After that they are returned to the carrier.
- Large items (over 25 kg or taller than 1.2 m) are not accepted. Arrange delivery to the loading dock with the office 48 hours ahead.

## Quiet hours
- Quiet hours are 22:00 to 7:00 on weeknights and 23:00 to 9:00 on Fridays and Saturdays.
- During quiet hours: no power tools, no loud music or parties, and no moving furniture in a way that is audible in other units.
- To report a noise problem, call the night line at ext. 199. Do not confront the neighbour directly.
- Planned renovation work is allowed Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00 only, with 5 days notice to the office.

## Other
- Trash chute is on every floor. Bulk trash pickup is the first Tuesday of the month, put items at the loading dock by 8:00.
- Maintenance requests go through the Larkspur app or by email to maintenance@larkspur-court.example. Emergencies (flood, no heat, gas smell): call ext. 199 any time.
