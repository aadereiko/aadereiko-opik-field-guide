# Lantern Hill Players: spring production notes (fictional)

Show: "The Lighthouse Keeper's Dinner", a two-act comedy. Opening night: Friday 22 May. Closing: Sunday 24 May.

## Roles
1. Morwenna Pell, the keeper (lead, about 60% of the lines)
2. Dr. Idris Vane, the visiting inspector (lead)
3. Tamsin, the cook (supporting)
4. Old Bartle, the fisherman (supporting, one scene in each act)
5. A Stranger, silent until the last scene (cameo, must wear a heavy coat)
