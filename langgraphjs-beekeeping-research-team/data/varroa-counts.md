# Varroa mite counts

Counts use a sticky board left under the open mesh floor for 3 days. The daily drop is the total mites divided by 3. Our action threshold in spring is 10 mites per day.

## North Apiary counts, 8 May
Hive N1 dropped 21 mites over 3 days, 7 per day. Hive N2 dropped 36 mites, 12 per day, above the threshold. Hive N3 dropped 9 mites, 3 per day, but the colony is too weak for treatment to matter.

## Orchard Apiary counts, 10 May
Hive O1 dropped 12 mites over 3 days, 4 per day. Hive O2 dropped 27 mites, 9 per day, just under the threshold, to be recounted in two weeks. Hive O3 dropped 45 mites, 15 per day, above the threshold.

## Treatment decisions
Hives over 10 mites per day get an oxalic acid vaporisation on a broodless day or a thymol strip course once supers are off. Hive N2 and Hive O3 were both booked for thymol strips after the first honey harvest on 20 June.
