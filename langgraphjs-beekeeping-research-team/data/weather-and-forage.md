# Weather and forage notes

## May weather summary
May was mild with eleven rainy days. Temperatures rarely dropped below 8 degrees at night. A cold snap on 14 May with 3 degrees at night slowed foraging for two days and pushed Hive N3 to the edge.

## Forage calendar
Apple blossom at the Orchard Apiary peaked on 3 to 12 May. Oilseed rape near the North Apiary flowered from 1 to 28 May. A June gap is expected between the rape and the lime trees, so weak colonies need watching then.

## Swarm season
The first swarm calls in the district came on 16 May. Hive N1 was the only one of our hives to show swarm preparations this spring.
