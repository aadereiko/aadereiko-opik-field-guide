# Feeding and stores

## Spring feeding record
Hive N3 received 2 litres of 1:1 sugar syrup on 4 May and again on 11 May. Hive N2 received 1 litre on 4 May. No other hive needed feeding because nectar was coming in.

## Winter stores check, 20 March
Hive N1 had about 14 kg of stores left. Hive N2 had 6 kg and was given a fondant block. Hive N3 had 3 kg and a fondant block. Hive O1 had 12 kg, Hive O2 had 9 kg and Hive O3 had 15 kg. A hive below 8 kg in March is considered at risk of starvation.

## Honey supers
Supers went on Hive N1, O1 and O3 on 25 May. Hive O2 got a super on 1 June after the chalkbrood spot recovered.
