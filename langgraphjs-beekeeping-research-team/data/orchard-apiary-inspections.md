# Orchard Apiary inspection log

## Hive O1 inspection, 6 May
Strong colony on 10 frames of bees. Queen seen and marked blue. Brood pattern excellent, about 95 percent of cells filled. Bees gentle. Three frames of nectar and one of pollen stored.

## Hive O2 inspection, 6 May
Medium colony on 8 frames of bees. Queen seen. Brood pattern good, 85 percent filled. Chalkbrood mummies noticed on the landing board, about a dozen. Entrance reduced and hive moved to a sunnier spot.

## Hive O3 inspection, 6 May
Strong colony on 12 frames of bees. Queen not seen, eggs present. Brood pattern excellent. A few cells with sunken, perforated cappings on frame 4, possible foulbrood. Ropiness test negative with a matchstick. Rechecked in a week.

## Hive O3 inspection, 13 May
Sunken cappings on frame 4 have cleared and no new ones appeared. Ropiness test negative again. Diagnosed as a harmless brood disturbance, not foulbrood. Colony growing well on 13 frames of bees.
