# North Apiary inspection log

## Hive N1 inspection, 4 May
Strong colony on 11 frames of bees. Queen seen, laying a tight pattern with eggs and young larvae in every frame of the brood nest. Two frames of capped stores in the super. Temperament calm. No queen cells.

## Hive N2 inspection, 4 May
Medium colony on 7 frames of bees. Queen not seen but fresh eggs present. Brood pattern spotty in the lower box, roughly 70 percent of cells filled. Bees slightly defensive. One supersedure cell found on frame 5 and left in place.

## Hive N3 inspection, 4 May
Weak colony on 4 frames of bees. No eggs, no young larvae, only a small patch of capped brood. Suspected queenless. Two laying worker signs: several eggs per cell. Recommended combining with N1 using the newspaper method.

## Hive N1 inspection, 18 May
Colony now fills two boxes, 14 frames of bees. Drawn comb everywhere, queen cells found on frames 3 and 8: preparing to swarm. Performed an artificial swarm split and moved the old queen with six frames to a nucleus box.
