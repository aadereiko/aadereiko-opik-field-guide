# Club Sites and Equipment Loans

## Observing sites
The club observes at two sites. Larkfield Common is a public field 12 miles north of town, open to members every clear Friday and Saturday evening. Pennywell Ridge is a farm site 31 miles west, with darker skies, and requires booking at least 3 days ahead through the site warden.

## Membership tiers
Junior membership costs 15 pounds a year and covers ages 12 to 17. Standard membership costs 40 pounds a year. Household membership costs 55 pounds a year and covers up to 4 people living at one address. Only Standard and Household members can borrow club equipment.

## Telescope loan scheme
Members can borrow one of the club's four loan telescopes for up to 21 days. The loan fleet is: a 6-inch Dobsonian, an 8-inch Dobsonian, a 90 mm refractor, and a 5-inch Maksutov. A refundable deposit of 50 pounds is held until the telescope is returned with all its caps and the Cheshire eyepiece.

## Late returns and damage
A telescope returned late loses 1 week of borrowing rights for each week it is overdue. If a mirror or lens is scratched or broken, the member covers the repair cost up to the 50 pound deposit, and the committee decides on anything above that. Report any damage the same day you notice it.

## Cancellations
If clouds are forecast, the Friday session is cancelled by 4 pm that day and announced in the members' chat group. Saturday sessions follow the same rule. Cancelled sessions are not rescheduled, but Pennywell Ridge bookings can move to any other date for free.
