# Brinewave BW-12 Dishwasher

## 1. Loading the racks
Load plates in the lower rack facing the centre of the machine. Place cups and glasses in the upper rack, tilted so water cannot pool in them. Do not let items block the rotating spray arms.

## 2. Wash programs
The BW-12 offers four programs: Eco takes 3 hours 20 minutes and uses 9 litres of water, Intensive takes 2 hours at 70 C for heavily soiled pans, Quick 30 washes lightly soiled dishes in 30 minutes, and Glass runs at 45 C to protect delicate stemware.

## 3. Detergent and rinse aid
Use one tablet or 20 grams of powder per cycle. Fill the rinse aid reservoir until the level window turns dark. If the RINSE light is on, the reservoir is almost empty and should be topped up within 5 cycles.

## 4. Salt for hard water
Add dishwasher salt to the salt container whenever the SALT light is on. Use only coarse dishwasher salt and never table salt, because table salt contains additives that clog the water softener. Set the water hardness dial to match your local supply.

## 5. Cleaning the filter
Clean the filter set once a month. Twist the coarse filter a quarter turn to the left, lift it out, and rinse all three filter parts under running water with a soft brush. Never run the machine without the filters fitted.

## 6. Troubleshooting
If the door will not close, check that nothing is sticking out of the lower rack. If water remains in the tub after a cycle, clean the filter set and check that the drain hose is not kinked. Error code F4 means a leak was detected: turn off the water supply and call Brinewave service.

## 7. Delay start
Press DELAY before closing the door to postpone the start by 3, 6 or 9 hours. The display counts down the hours remaining. Opening the door cancels the delay.
