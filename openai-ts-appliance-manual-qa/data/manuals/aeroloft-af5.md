# Aeroloft AF-5 Air Fryer

## 1. First use
Wash the basket and crisper plate in warm soapy water. Run the empty fryer at 200 C for 10 minutes to burn off any manufacturing residue. A light odour during this first run is normal.

## 2. Preheating
Preheat for 3 minutes at the cooking temperature before adding food. Press the PREHEAT button and wait for the double beep. Food added to a cold basket cooks unevenly.

## 3. Basket capacity
The basket holds up to 1.2 kg of fries. Do not fill past the MAX mark, because crowded food does not crisp. Shake the basket halfway through the cooking time for even results.

## 4. Cooking guide
Frozen fries cook at 200 C for 18 minutes. Chicken drumsticks cook at 180 C for 25 minutes and should reach an internal temperature of 75 C. Salmon fillets cook at 160 C for 10 minutes. Vegetables such as broccoli cook at 180 C for 8 minutes.

## 5. Cleaning
Unplug the fryer and let it cool for 30 minutes before cleaning. The basket and crisper plate are dishwasher safe. Wipe the heating element with a damp cloth and never pour water into the fryer body.

## 6. Troubleshooting
If white smoke comes out, there is too much grease in the basket: pour away excess oil and wash the basket. If the fryer will not start, check that the basket is fully pushed in, because the safety switch only closes when the basket is seated. Error code H1 means the fryer overheated and shut itself off. Wait 15 minutes before restarting.

## 7. Using foil and paper
Use only perforated baking paper made for air fryers. Never cover the whole crisper plate, because blocked airflow causes overheating. Make sure paper is weighed down by food so it cannot touch the heating element.
