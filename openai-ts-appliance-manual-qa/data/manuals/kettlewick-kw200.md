# Kettlewick KW-200 Smart Kettle

## 1. Before first use
Fill the kettle to the MAX line and boil it once, then pour the water away. Repeat the rinse boil a second time. This removes the factory film and any metallic smell from the heating plate.

## 2. Temperature presets
Press the TEMP button to cycle through the presets: 70 C for baby formula, 80 C for green tea, 90 C for French press coffee and 100 C for black tea. The ring around the dial glows blue while the kettle heats and turns white when the target is reached.

## 3. Keep-warm mode
Hold the TEMP button for three seconds to start keep-warm mode. The kettle holds the chosen temperature for 30 minutes and then switches itself off. Keep-warm cannot be used with less than 0.5 litres of water.

## 4. Descaling
Descale the kettle every 4 weeks in hard-water areas. Fill it halfway with water, add 2 tablespoons of citric acid, boil, and leave the solution to stand for 20 minutes before rinsing. Never use vinegar, because it can damage the silicone seal around the lid.

## 5. Error codes
E1 means the kettle was switched on empty. Let the base cool for 10 minutes, add water and try again. E2 means the temperature sensor has failed. Unplug the kettle and contact Kettlewick support, because the unit cannot be repaired at home.

## 6. Safety
Do not immerse the base or the power cord in water. Always lift the kettle off its base before pouring. The exterior stays cool to the touch, but the lid and spout become hot during boiling.

## 7. Warranty
The KW-200 comes with a 2 year limited warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Scale damage caused by skipping descaling is not covered.
